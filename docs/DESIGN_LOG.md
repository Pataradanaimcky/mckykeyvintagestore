## Decisions (settled by the owner)

- Product lines: packs for Yamaha MODX M (.Y2L) and Roland FANTOM-0 (.SVZ), Thai Pop / City Pop.
- The owner's face does not appear on the site or in demos.
- Pack names, prices and counts are sample data. The owner replaces them himself. Do not ask about this.
- Store layout follows a retail catalog pattern: promo banner, category tabs, product tile grid. The owner pointed to a Lenskart listing page as the reference for this direction.
- The site is in Thai.
- Payments stay simulated until the owner chooses a gateway.
- Buyers must see at a glance which keyboard each pack is for. Every pack carries a plain-text "สำหรับ <maker> <model>" label, and the store front filters by instrument.
- The owner approved this direction on 2026-11-11 ("เอาอันนี้"). Refine it; do not replace it. Colors are black, blue and red only: near-black page, white text, blue for the MODX M line and red for the FANTOM-0 line. No yellow, no other accent.
- Current direction: a modern studio-style page. Very large tight headlines, lots of empty space, white pill buttons on the black page, large rounded cards, one typeface (Anuphan). His reference for the feel and the animation is the Arounda agency site; take the general approach only, never its layout, text or artwork.
- The store is themed around two product lines, and each has its own color used on its hero card, pack covers and chip dot: Yamaha MODX M (blue) and Roland FANTOM-0 (red). These are the store's own line colors, not the makers' brand colors. Do not add a third accent color.
- Motion is part of the look: headline lines slide up from a mask on load, sections fade up on scroll (RevealObserver + `data-reveal`), a large marquee of sound names, cards zoom their cover and show an arrow on hover, keys play on hover, button arrows slide. Keep motion smooth and slow (the `--ease-out-soft` curve). Everything stops for reduced-motion users and nothing is hidden without JavaScript.
- Do not bring back: dark hero with grid or glow, neon accents, monospace "terminal" text, drawn synth panels with knobs. The owner rejected those as looking AI-made.
- Real photos beat drawn covers. Set `image` on a product to show a photo from `/public/packs/`. Only use the owner's own photos of his own keyboards.
- The store keeps its own identity and must not imitate Yamaha's or Roland's corporate identity. No maker logos, wordmarks, brand color schemes, product photos from their sites, or drawings of their real products. Maker and model names appear only as plain text saying what a pack works with, never inside a pack's name, and the footer trademark note stays.
- The site is made of separate pages, not one long page with jump links: home, /packs, /packs/[slug], /cart, /checkout, /compatibility, /faq, /login, /account. Menu links go to pages. Keep it that way.
- The brand mark is three white keys with a blue and a red short key on black (`BrandMark.tsx`, `public/brand/`). Do not redraw or recolor it.
- 2026-11-12, owner request: the store has a black theme and a white theme and follows the visitor's phone or computer setting (`prefers-color-scheme`). Black stays the default. The white theme is slightly warm, like old paper. Blue and red stay the only accent colors in both themes.
- 2026-11-12, owner request: the top of the home page is a playable synth rack (`src/components/rack`). Visitors move patch cables between jacks, turn knobs and play a one-octave keyboard (mouse, touch, or computer keys A to K), so they stay on the site longer. This is the one place drawn panels with knobs are allowed; pack covers and everything else still follow the earlier rules. The rack is the store's own generic drawing with plain labels (SEQ, OSC, LFO, FILTER, OUT, KEYS) and must not copy a real module, maker name or logo. Sound never starts on its own: only the sound switch or a key press starts it.
- 2026-11-12, owner request: the feel is vintage, like the reference photos he sent (a cream-panel modular rack with colored cables, a black modular rack, an old polysynth and drum machine on a wooden floor, a film photo of a musician with old gear by the sea at dusk). Cream faceplates, film grain, hanging cables. Take the mood only.
- 2026-11-12, owner request: more animation is welcome, still smooth, and still off for reduced-motion users.
- 2026-11-12, owner request: the rack is a set of pages you can swipe between (or pick from tabs): MODULAR, FM 6-OP and POLY. The FM page plays DX7 voice files (.syx) with a DX7-compatible engine; the POLY page is an 80s-style analog polysynth. Page names are generic; "DX7" appears only as plain text describing the file format. Only the page in view listens to the computer keyboard or makes sound. Use only engines with permissive licences (MIT, Apache, BSD); do not copy GPL code such as Dexed or JunoX into the site. Do not ship Yamaha factory voices.
- 2026-11-12, owner request: each rack page has its own instrument finish so the engines feel different. MODULAR keeps the cream panels. FM 6-OP is a cool graphite digital panel with a screen, flat buttons and operator faders. POLY is a black panel with cream print, wooden end cheeks and sliders. These are the store's own designs in the spirit of each era; do not copy the front panel, colors or button layout of a real instrument (DX7, Juno or any other).
- 2026-11-12, owner direction: selling is no longer the only point. The site is also a tool: /studio lets people arrange the parts of a layered sound for their own keyboard (MODX M, FANTOM-0, XPS-30, XPS-10, D-50, with the FM and POLY engines as preview sounds), more easily than on the keyboard's small screen. It should feel like arranging on that keyboard (its own words, its part count, its note naming) but simpler. Sounds can be previewed in the browser or played on the keyboard over USB, and the result downloaded. The store stays. "สตูดิโอ" is the first menu link. Details and the research checklist: docs/STUDIO.md.
- 2026-11-12, owner request: build the studio in this order: DX7 first (to learn and get the full loop working), then MODX M, then FANTOM-0. The studio opens on the DX7 editor. The goal is that people who use the free tools also buy the store's packs.
- 2026-11-12, owner correction (replaces the two /studio entries above): everything else stays as it was (store, playable rack). /studio is a sound organizer, menu "จัดเรียงเสียง": people edit sounds and parts on their keyboard, then come here only to put their sounds in order (DX7 cartridge from .syx, MODX M Live Set, FANTOM-0 scenes). No part arranging and no sound editing on the site. Order of work: DX7, then MODX M, then FANTOM-0.
- 2026-11-12, owner request: the home page is a landing page for the three things the site does, in this order: 01 play the synths (the three-page rack lives on the landing page), 02 arrange keyboard sounds (/studio is a feature page, not the main page), 03 buy sound packs. The headline and three linked cards at the top say this; each part then has its own section. Menu order: แพ็กเสียง, จัดเรียงเสียง, รุ่นที่ใช้ได้, คำถาม, บัญชี.
- 2026-11-12, owner request: every synth on the site has default sounds to play, and there are free packs people can take and play, like the default banks that come with open-source synths. All default and free sounds are the store's own; no maker factory sounds.
- 2026-11-12, owner request: the organizer shows each keyboard as a machine face in the store's own style (screen, voice buttons or pads, a cartridge slot for DX7), not a copy of the maker's panel. Choosing a sound must feel like choosing it on the instrument: the button lights and bounces, the screen updates, and DX7 voices play at once. The DX7 cartridge starts loaded with the store's free MCKY FM bank.
- 2026-11-12, owner request: make the MODX M organizer work like the keyboard's Live Set. The owner offered to allow copying the MODX M design; that permission is not his to give (Yamaha's design), so the face stays the store's own drawing, but the workflow, words, numbers, colors and jobs follow the keyboard exactly.
- 2026-11-12, owner request: use open source to let people hear MODX M sounds on the site where possible. The real sounds cannot be emulated (Yamaha samples, undocumented files), so slots preview with openly licensed General MIDI samples of the same type, clearly labelled as close matches.
- 2026-11-12, owner request: a demo page where people hear sample sounds and patches, can swap between them, and cannot download them. /demo, menu "ฟังเดโม".
- 2026-11-12, owner clarification: "demo" means demo banks in the organizer: a store pack's sounds load onto the keyboard face, each slot plays its own demo, people can swap and arrange, but nothing from a demo bank can be downloaded, saved or shared.
- 2026-11-12, owner request (replaces the /demo page entry above): no separate demo page; demos live inside each feature (▶ on every sound in a pack page, demo banks in the organizer). Navigation must be easy: the menu shows the three parts in the landing page order, numbered 01 เล่นซินธ์ (/#play), 02 จัดเรียงเสียง (/studio), 03 แพ็กเสียง (/packs), then บัญชี; รุ่นที่ใช้ได้ and คำถาม move to the footer. On a phone the menu is one row that scrolls sideways under the name, with the cart beside the name.

## Decisions by the project manager

Delegated by the owner on 2026-11-12. The PM records each decision here with the date and the question it answers. Agents treat these as settled. The PM never decides the matters reserved for the owner in AGENTS.md ("Who decides"), and never edits the owner's Decisions above.

- 2026-11-13: MODX M Live Set slots of type Song, Audio or Pattern: keep option (a), one note on the page; the organizer arranges Perform slots only. Reason: keeps the tool simple and stays a sound organizer, not an editor.
- 2026-11-13: FANTOM-0 reading from the keyboard: keep option (a), one read-only press per scene on screen, no Bank Select or Program Change from the site. Reason: never changes the keyboard's state or loses unsaved edits.
- 2026-11-13: The synth rack stays on the home page only (answers the Open question). Reason: it brings players in without pulling attention from buying on the store pages.
- 2026-11-13: No new features that send MIDI to a keyboard until the owner or a tester has tried the existing read-only MODX M and FANTOM-0 buttons on a real keyboard; researcher work goes to items checkable from manuals and to a short real-keyboard test script for the owner. Reason: untested USB features pile up risk without bringing buyers.
- 2026-11-14: FANTOM-0 item 5e (Receive Exclusive default, Device ID name and range), researcher question of 2026-11-13: option (b), leave 5e to the owner's real-keyboard test, which already asks for it; the researcher stops retrying 5e and agents do not accept Roland's download terms. Reason: costs nothing, needs no legal call, and the test answers it for sure.
- 2026-11-14: How much manual detail goes on /studio: new manual facts go into docs/STUDIO.md and the owner's test script first; the page gets a new note only when it changes what the visitor presses, and the steps come before the notes. Reason: the help text has grown every hour, and a visitor on a phone should reach the demo banks and packs quickly.
