# MODX M and FANTOM-0 simulators

Owner request, 2026-11-16: the site has no MODX M or FANTOM simulator; he wants one, and if there is no ready-made one, a way must be found. A dedicated scheduled task, "Mcky keyboard simulator builder", builds it. The project manager ("Mcky project manager") sets its priorities in `docs/ROADMAP.md` (Next, items marked "simulator agent") and answers its questions like the other agents (AGENTS.md, "Who decides").

## What "simulator" means here (read first)

- **No exact copy is possible or allowed.** The real sounds are the makers' own samples and data (Yamaha AWM2 waveforms, Roland ZEN-Core PCM). They cannot be downloaded, extracted or redistributed, and there is no official browser version of either keyboard. Never ship, extract or reverse-engineer factory sounds, waveforms or firmware; never copy the panel or screen design (owner decision of 2026-11-12, MODX M entry in DESIGN_LOG).
- **What we can build:** a page in the home-page rack for each keyboard that works the way the keyboard works and is played with our own engines and openly licensed samples:
  - **MODX M style:** a Performance of up to 8 keyboard parts (layer and split by key range), sample, FM and analog-style engines like the keyboard's three engines, a Super Knob that moves several parameters at once, and 8 Scenes that recall part settings.
  - **FANTOM-0 style:** a Scene with zones (layer and split), tones made of partials (sample, analog-style, supersaw, noise), and 16 pads.
  - The face is the store's own drawing, as on /studio. Labels say plainly that it is an emulation and not the keyboard's real sound, for example "จำลองการทำงานแบบ MODX M · ไม่ใช่เสียงจริงของเครื่อง". Maker names are plain text only, as in the footer trademark note.
- **Why it sells packs:** players learn the workflow on the site, hear the store's own sounds built for the simulator, and buy the real packs for their keyboard. Simulator versions of the store's packs are the owner's to approve (Waiting on the owner).

## Owner request 2026-11-16 (second message): feel like the real keyboard, and connect to it

**Face.** The owner wants playing the simulator to feel like playing a real MODX M or FANTOM-0. Do this through *function and placement*, not by copying the look:
- Same controls, in the same places relative to each other, doing the same jobs, with the keyboard's own words on them. MODX M: part sliders and knobs, the Super Knob, Scene buttons, part select/mute/solo, a display showing what the keyboard's display shows (Performance name, parts, Scene). FANTOM-0: zone sliders and knobs, the pads, Scene select, zone on/off, a display with Scene and zones.
- The same steps to do things (e.g. pick a part, then turn its knobs; press a Scene button to recall). A player who knows the keyboard should know where to reach without reading.
- The appearance stays the store's own drawing (shapes, colors, type, no maker logos, no copy of the panel's styling or the screen's graphics), like /studio. Yamaha's and Roland's panel designs are theirs; the owner's earlier permission is not his to give (DESIGN_LOG, 2026-11-12). Copy the layout of controls and the workflow, never the styling.

**Connect to the real keyboard (Web MIDI, USB).** Works in Chrome and Edge (desktop and Android); say plainly on the page when the browser has no Web MIDI (e.g. Safari on iPhone). Only messages printed in the maker's own MIDI documentation (MODX M / MONTAGE M Data List and MIDI implementation; FANTOM-0 MIDI implementation). Add each message with its source page to this file before using it.
- **In (keyboard → site):** play the simulator from the real keyboard's keys, wheels, sustain and knobs/sliders (notes, pitch bend, CCs). Learn which CC each control sends from the manual, with a "learn" fallback (turn a control, the site picks it up).
- **Out (site → keyboard), live control only:** select a Performance / Scene on the real keyboard (Bank Select + Program Change as documented), send knob/slider CCs, all-notes-off. Nothing that writes or overwrites the keyboard's memory.
- **Writing to the keyboard's memory:** not part of the simulator. Only if the maker documents it, behind a clear confirmation that is off by default and says it is untested on a real keyboard (same rule as /studio).
- Everything is labelled "ยังไม่ได้ทดสอบกับเครื่องจริง" until the owner tries it on his keyboards (Waiting on the owner).

## Confirmed facts (with sources)

- MIDI in, read side (S8): only the general MIDI 1.0 channel messages are read: Note On (9n), Note Off (8n, or 9n with velocity 0), Control Change (Bn), Pitch Bend (En, 14 bits, 8192 = centre), sustain = CC 64; CC 0/32 (Bank Select) and 120–127 (channel mode) are not learnt. Source: MIDI Association, [Summary of MIDI 1.0 Messages](https://midi.org/summary-of-midi-1-0-messages) and [Control Change messages](https://midi.org/midi-1-0-control-change-messages). Nothing is sent to the keyboard.

- MODX M (announced Oct 2025): 8 faders (MODX+ had 4); Super Knob with three Knob Position buttons (Left, Mid, Right); dedicated part-select and Scene-select buttons (counts not stated); six knobs under the touchscreen. Engines: AWM2 (128 elements per part), FM-X (8 operators, 88 algorithms), AN-X (3 oscillators with 5 waveforms, noise, 2 filters with 10 types each, 1 amp envelope, 2 LFOs). Polyphony 268. Source: [Synth Anatomy, Oct 2025](https://synthanatomy.com/2025/10/yamaha-modx-m-hardware-synthesizer-gets-major-hands-on-upgrade-with-an-x-engine.html); announcement: [Yamaha press release](https://usa.yamaha.com/news_events/2025/yamaha-unveils-modx-m-synthesizer.html). Still to confirm from Yamaha's manual: number of Scene buttons (the page uses 8), number of parts the faders control, panel positions.
- MODX M top panel, from the [MODX M Quick Guide (Yamaha, data.yamaha.com)](https://data.yamaha.com/files/download/other_assets/0/2575110/MODX-M_quick_guide_En_B0.pdf) (read 2026-11-16), "Real-time control" items in Yamaha's numbered order: 5-1 [QUICK EDIT], 5-2 [ASSIGN], 5-3 Knobs 1–4 (5–8), 5-4 [PAGE JUMP], 5-5 Super Knob, 5-6 KNOB POSITION buttons, 5-7 Slider function [PART]/[EL/OP/OSC], 5-8 Control sliders 1–8, 5-9 Part group [1-8/9-16]/[AUDIO], 5-10 PART buttons (EL/OP/OSC), 5-11 [COMMON], 5-12 [PART SELECT] (MUTE), 5-13 [SHIFT], 5-14 OCTAVE (TRANSPOSE), 5-15 SCENE buttons (STORE), 5-16 [MASTER VOLUME] … 5-22 pitch bend wheel, 5-23 modulation wheel, 5-24 [ASSIGN 1]/[ASSIGN 2], 5-28 [CONTROL ASSIGN]. The guide numbers items but does not say where each sits left to right (unknown).
  - Super Knob controls "multiple parameters (Assign 1–8) assigned to knobs 1–4 (5–8)"; KNOB POSITION [LEFT] [MID] [RIGHT] recall a stored Super Knob setting; [SHIFT] + a KNOB POSITION button saves the current values to it (p. 23).
  - Control sliders 1–8 set the volume of Parts 1–16 (1–8 / 9–16, switched by the Part group button), or element / operator / oscillator / drum key levels with [EL/OP/OSC] (p. 24).
  - PART buttons select Parts 1–8 (or 9–16); [SHIFT] + [PART SELECT] sets the Part's MUTE on or off (p. 25). No separate MUTE or SOLO button is listed.
  - SCENE buttons recall "snapshots" of Part settings such as mute and mixing; [SHIFT] + SCENE stores the Scene; edited Scenes are lost unless stored before switching or power off (p. 25). The guide does not print the number of SCENE buttons; the Operation Manual copy (p. 18) says Scene 1–8 (not yet seen on a Yamaha-hosted page).
  - A Performance has up to 16 Parts; [SPLIT] opens the NOTE RANGE screen for split and layer (pp. 14, 19). Engines: AWM2 (sampled waves), FM-X (modulating waveforms), AN-X (simple waveforms with modelling filters) (p. 9); FM-X has 8 operators and 88 algorithms, AN-X 3 oscillators and noise (Operation Manual copy, pp. 6–7).
  - Unknown: panel positions left to right, how many PART buttons there are, what the Super Knob's Assign 1–8 are by default.
- FANTOM-0 (2022): 61 / 76 / 88-key models, colour touchscreen, RGB pads, Scenes, ZEN-Core engine. The press release gives no counts for knobs, sliders, pads, zones or partials. Source: [Roland press release](https://www.roland.com/us/company/press_releases/2022/Roland-Introduces-FANTOM-0-Synthesizer-Keyboard-Se/).
- FANTOM-0 top panel and structure, from the [FANTOM-06/07/08 Owner's Manual (Roland, static.roland.com)](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/FANTOM-06_07_08_eng01_W.pdf) (read 2026-11-16):
  - Panel (pp. 8–9; the manual numbers sections but does not say left/right): Control knobs [1]–[8]; Sliders [1]–[8]; [ZONE SELECT] selects the current zone; ZONE INT/EXT buttons [1]–[8] set whether each zone sounds; [ZONE 9-16] switches the buttons to zones 9–16; [SCENE SELECT] opens the SCENE SELECT screen; Pads [1]–[16]; [PAD MODE] sets what the pads do.
  - Knobs and sliders (pp. 8, 19): with PAN/LEVEL on, knobs [1]–[8] set each zone's pan and sliders [1]–[8] each zone's volume. With CTRL on, the knobs control the current zone: ATTACK, RELEASE, REVERB, CHORUS, LOW GAIN, MID GAIN, HIGH GAIN, EQ ON. ASSIGN1 uses parameters set for the scene, ASSIGN2 those set in system settings. Motional pad: drag on the screen to change the volume of four zones at once.
  - Structure (p. 16): a Scene holds the playing state, including each zone's tone, MFX and volume; there are 16 zones, combined to layer tones; a tone is the smallest unit of sound; some tones are drum kits.
  - Layer and split (p. 17): zones whose ZONE INT/EXT button is lit red sound as layers; hold [SPLIT] and press a key to set the split point; the high side plays zone 1, the low side zone 4, and the split key belongs to zone 1. Key ranges per zone with KEY RANGE. Transpose −5…+6 semitones, octave ±3 (p. 18).
  - Pads (pp. 23–24): the pads can select scenes or play samples (SAMPLE PAD is one mode); the list of pad modes is in the Reference Manual (unknown here).
- ZEN-Core tone (from Roland's [ZENOLOGY Owner's Manual](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/ZENOLOGY_Manual_eng09_W.pdf), same ZEN-Core engine; the FANTOM-0 Owner's Manual does not print a partial count): 4 partials, each on/off; OSC types PCM, VA, PCM-Sync, SuperSAW, Noise.
- FANTOM-0 Reference Manual, Parameter Guide and MIDI Implementation are listed on [Roland's FANTOM-07 manuals page](https://www.roland.com/us/support/by_product/fantom-07/owners_manuals/), but the download sits behind a licence-agreement checkbox the agent cannot tick, so their contents are not read this run. Unknown: pad mode list, zone velocity range, number of scenes per SCENE SELECT screen as a fact for the simulator (STUDIO.md cites 4 × 4 from the Owner's Manual p. 17), MIDI messages.
